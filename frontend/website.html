System Engineering

Tri-Spectral Forensic Pipeline

Single-modality tools fail on sophisticated deepfakes. DeepGuard AI decomposes media into three independent physical channels and correlates their mutual consistency.

📷
Branch 1: Spatial & Optical Vision
Extracts true physical camera sensor noise, 2D Fast Fourier Transform high-frequency spikes, and facial boundary blending seams.
  • Laplacian photon noise variance
  • Error Level Analysis (ELA 90% delta)
  • 2D FFT upsampling grid detection
  • Vision Transformer (ViT) latent inference
🎙️
Branch 2: Acoustic Neural Vocoder
Scans audio waveforms for transposed convolution artifacts, unnatural pitch stability, and lack of biological vocal cord micro-tremors.
  • Spectral centroid & rolloff dispersion
  • Neural vocoder high-frequency cutoff
  • Fundamental frequency (F0) micro-jitter
  • Harmonic-to-noise ratio (HNR)
🗣️
Branch 3: Cross-Modal Speech Synchrony
Transcribes speech using Whisper ASR and cross-correlates audio phoneme timings against visual lip and facial landmark movements.
  • Whisper ASR phoneme timestamping
  • Visual-audio temporal agreement
  • Disagreement matrix (Face-swap vs Voice-clone)
  • Tamper-evident correlation score
