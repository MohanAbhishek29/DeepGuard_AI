Pipeline Architecture

Three Independent Forensic Signals. One Verdict.

Unlike single-modality classifiers, DeepGuard AI runs three completely independent analysis branches on every uploaded file, then cross-correlates their outputs to catch disagreements that betray manipulation.

01
Vision Branch — Optical Sensor Physics
Extracts Laplacian texture variance, 2D-FFT spatial frequency ratios, and facial boundary seam discontinuities using OpenCV. Detects diffusion over-smoothing (Midjourney, SDXL, Flux) and face-swap splices (DeepFaceLab, Roop, FaceFusion).
CV + ELA + FFT
02
Audio Branch — Neural Vocoder Detection
Runs 1D-FFT across the 16–22 kHz band. HiFi-GAN, SoundStream, and EnCodec neural vocoders leave unnatural high-frequency energy hash. Suno AI embeds ID3 watermarks detected by tag inspection.
1D-FFT + ID3 Tags
03
Speech Branch — Phoneme Synchrony
Whisper ASR transcribes the audio stream and validates phoneme-to-frame alignment. Synthetic speech shows quantized micro-pauses and uniform formant transitions absent in natural vocal cord production.
Whisper ASR + Formants
04
Cross-Modal Correlator — Final Verdict Engine
Combines all three branch scores. Detects cross-modal disagreement — e.g. authentic video with cloned voice (Real Video + Cloned Voice = MANIPULATED) or deepfake face with real audio. Issues forensic audit certificate with SHA-256 file hash.
Bayesian Fusion + SHA-256

Real-World Application

Where DeepGuard Catches What Others Miss

USE CASE — 01
Political Disinformation
AI-generated videos of political leaders making false statements spread within hours of creation. Standard fact-checkers lack the technical tools to flag them before viral spread.
Scenario: A deepfake video of a politician announcing policy reversal — DeepGuard detects via facial boundary seam ratio >1.35 and clean 25.000 FPS (no hardware clock drift) → MANIPULATED
USE CASE — 02
Academic Fraud Detection
Students submitting AI-generated photographs as original creative work for assignments. The images look indistinguishable from real photos to untrained human reviewers.
Scenario: Midjourney portrait submitted as original photography — DeepGuard detects via ELA std <3.5, no Make/Model EXIF, FFT ratio >1.38 (3/7 signals) → SUSPICIOUS
USE CASE — 03
Media Authenticity Verification
News agencies need to verify uploaded media before publication. A single published deepfake can destroy editorial credibility. Manual review at scale is impossible.
Scenario: Real phone video with AI-cloned voice overlay — Vision branch returns AUTHENTIC (fps_drift=true), Audio branch returns SUSPICIOUS (HF ratio=0.012) → Cross-modal: CLONED VOICE DETECTED
