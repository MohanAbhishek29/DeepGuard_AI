Section 01

How DeepGuard AI Detects Synthetic Media

Every analysis runs three independent evidence pipelines simultaneously. The final verdict is a Bayesian combination of all physical and neural signals — no single point of failure, no filename guessing.

01

Vision Forensics — Image & Video

Neural + Physical

A fine-tuned Vision Transformer (ViT-Base) runs multi-scale Test-Time Augmentation at three resolutions (full frame, 80% center crop, 2× upsampled) and ensembles predictions to catch ultra-realistic Gemini / DALL-E 3 / Flux 8K images that fool single-pass classifiers. Combined with:

  • Error Level Analysis (ELA) — AI images have unnaturally uniform JPEG recompression residual (Std < 2.8) unlike optical sensor noise in real photos.
  • 2D-FFT Spatial Frequency Analysis — Diffusion model upsampling creates transposed convolution checkerboard grids detectable in the frequency domain.
  • Laplacian Variance (Sensor Grain) — Real cameras produce organic photon noise. Generative models airbrush skin, producing unnatural smoothness.
  • Face Seam Boundary Analysis — Face-swap compositing creates measurable sharpness discontinuity at the facial boundary region.
  • EXIF Hardware Provenance — Camera Make/Model/ISO tags prove optical sensor origin. AI images carry no hardware chain.
  • Generative Resolution Buckets — Midjourney, SDXL, Flux, and Bing DALL-E always output specific pixel dimensions (1024×1024, 1344×768, etc.).
02

Acoustic Forensics — Voice & Audio

1D-FFT + Metadata

Neural vocoders (HiFi-GAN, SoundStream, Bark) used by Suno AI, ElevenLabs, and RVC leave distinctive high-frequency energy signatures above 16 kHz — absent in natural analog microphone recordings.

  • High-Frequency Vocoder Ratio (>16kHz) — Authentic human voices have smooth physical roll-off. AI vocoders inject a quantization hash in the ultrasonic band.
  • Pitch Micro-Jitter Analysis — Human vocal cords produce natural micro-tremors (jitter ≈ 0.012). Synthetic TTS outputs are unnaturally steady (jitter ≈ 0.001).
  • Container Watermark Scan — Suno AI and Udio embed "made with suno" in ID3 comment tags — audited at the binary container level.
  • 128-Band Mel Spectrogram — Multi-channel spectral decomposition identifies artificial frequency band boundaries from neural audio codecs.
03

Cross-Modal Correlation — Video

Multi-Signal Fusion

For video files, vision and audio evidence streams are cross-correlated to detect four distinct manipulation scenarios: authentic, visual deepfake (real voice / fake face), voice clone (real video / dubbed audio), and fully synthetic. Disagreement between modalities is itself a forensic indicator.

  • Modality Agreement Matrix — Independent vision and audio verdicts are compared; a discrepancy (e.g. real face + cloned voice) triggers a targeted alert.
  • Temporal Timeline Events — Anomalies are timestamped at their occurrence within the video for frame-accurate forensic reporting.
  • Confidence Bayesian Fusion — Weighted probability combination across all 10 signal layers produces a final confidence percentage from 0–100%.
